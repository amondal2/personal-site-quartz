Welcome to my site! I am an incoming research fellow at [McGill University](https://www.mcgill.ca/) and the [Quebec AI Institute](https://mila.quebec/en).
Broadly, I'm interested in using machine learning and high-performance computing to improve disease detection and health outcomes. 

I completed my [PhD](https://escholarship.org/content/qt9t88k9vw/qt9t88k9vw.pdf) from the University of California, Berkeley in 2024. I also worked at the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention's [Center for Forecasting and Outbreak Analytics](https://www.cdc.gov/forecast-outbreak-analytics/index.html), developing disease-burden estimates and strengthening computing infrastructure for public health response. My full CV is available [here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/199IReBPNmGOZfmFr2CKkVxzJ9LNYYitV/view?usp=sharing).

Feel free to [contact me](mailto:agastya.mondal@gmail.com).
